# Third-party files

These binaries are included unchanged and are licensed under the GNU GPL
(version 2 or later). Author: Pete Batard and contributors.

- `uefi-ntfs.img`: UEFI:NTFS boot loader with NTFS / exFAT drivers (Secure Boot signed).
- `md5/*.efi`: uefi-md5sum boot loaders used by UEFI:Validation.
- `setup/setup_x64.exe`, `setup/setup_arm64.exe`: Windows 11 Setup wrapper.

Source code: https://github.com/pbatard/uefi-ntfs , https://github.com/pbatard/uefi-md5sum
